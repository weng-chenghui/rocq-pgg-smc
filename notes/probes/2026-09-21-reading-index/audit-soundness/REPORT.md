## What I checked and found clean

The attack model is untouched: the threshold hypothesis `#|C| < profile_k (instance_profile A)` is unchanged at every site of the staged diff, and a reading acts only on the coalition's endpoint map. `var_dist_fdistmap_transfer` (`security/pgg_collusion_bound.v:987`) was already stated for arbitrary maps, so no frozen file needs generalising and invariant 7 holds. `k12_k14_psl211.v` smuggles nothing, and `assumptions-k14.txt` reports `propositional_extensionality`, `functional_extensionality_dep` and `constructive_indefinite_description` only. The K5 copies are verbatim against `2fc0108`. The `of r` clause really is a type ascription: the notation elaborates to `@exact_of_reading (tableau_at s) r w`, so a witness at another reading is refused where it is written. The obstruction constructor takes the reading and the number at different types, so a swapped pair is a type error, as `chains.log` records happening once during the migration.
