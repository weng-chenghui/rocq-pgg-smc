# Proposal: PSL(2,11) on twelve colour-only cards, privacy 5 from design strength

Date: 2026-09-14

One-line summary: twelve cards, six hearts and six clubs, shuffled by the group
generated by two label-free operations, "reverse each quarter" and "Monge-shuffle
each half". That group is PSL(2,11), order 660, only 2-transitive, yet every
coalition of at most five cards learns nothing about the secret "which of the
two mirror-image Steiner systems S(5,6,12) holds the heart positions", because
both secret classes are 5-designs. It reaches the M12 privacy rung with a group
144 times smaller, an enumerated word certificate of the pgl27_mixing.v shape,
and generators that are the M12 shuffles applied to sub-piles.

## 1. The search constraints and what they ruled out

Constraints accumulated over the two search rounds:

- C1 a two-class (or more) secret carried by an orbit invariant of the shuffle
  group, with a privacy threshold k >= 3;
- C2 generators that are physical card operations, not the PGL translation /
  scaling / inversion triple;
- C3 an in-kernel enumerated word certificate at some computable length L
  (group order in the thousands at most, L in the hundreds);
- C4 different enough from PGL(2,7) to carry a paper, not a footnote.

Ruled out, with the measured reason:

| candidate | fails | measured reason |
|---|---|---|
| M12 (Monge + reversal, 12 cards) | C3 | 95040 states; Monge alphabets mix at 0.04-0.06 bit per letter, L about 700-1000 for 2^-40 |
| M11 on 11 cards | C2 | no involution of M11 is a block reversal, no contiguous sub-pile cut, faro or Monge lies in M11; the second generator is a table |
| PGL(2,9), PGL(2,11), PGL(2,13), M10 | C2 | the same generator triple (L = 225 / 225 / 245 would have been fine) |
| AGL(3,2) on 8 cards (faro + one swap) | C4 | same deck, same threshold 3 as PGL(2,7); 14 / 56 against 28 / 42 |
| PGL(2,8), PGammaL(2,8), S_n, A_n | C1 | one orbit on (t+1)-subsets, nothing to hide |
| faro groups <I,O> on 8, 10, 12 cards | C1 | orders 24, 1920, 7680, imprimitive; every subset orbit has design strength 1, so k = 1 |
| Monge group <u,z> on 8 cards | C1 | order 32, strength 1 |
| cyclic C_7, C_11 (one cut) | C1 | difference-set orbits give k = 2 and no more |

## 2. The criterion that replaces transitivity

Under the all-decks dealer, and under the fixed-representative dealer when
cards of one colour are indistinguishable, a coalition C sees the secret only
through the law of H cap C, H the heart set. If H is drawn from an orbit that
is a t-design, the number of blocks meeting C in a prescribed A subset of C is
fixed by the design parameters for |C| <= t, and lambda_t / b = C(h,t) / C(v,t)
does not depend on the orbit. So two secret classes that are both t-designs
of the same block size give identical coalition laws up to size t, whatever
the tuple-transitivity of the group. Privacy threshold of a secret pair =
min design strength of the two orbits.

Census with this criterion (scratch `notes/probes/design_census.py`), best
secret pair per group:

| group | order | tuple t | subset orbits (size, strength) | k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PGL(2,7) on 8, h = 4 | 336 | 3 | (42,3) (28,3) | 3 |
| PSL(2,7) on 8, h = 4 | 168 | 2 | (42,3) (14,3) (14,3) | 3 |
| AGL(3,2) on 8, h = 4 | 1344 | 3 | (56,3) (14,3) | 3 |
| M11 on 11, h = 5 | 7920 | 4 | (396,4) (66,4) | 4 |
| PSL(2,11) on 12, h = 4 | 660 | 2 | (330,3) (165,3) | 3 |
| **PSL(2,11) on 12, h = 6** | **660** | **2** | **(132,5) (132,5)** (330,3) (110,3) x3 | **5** |
| M12 on 12, h = 6 | 95040 | 5 | (792,5) (132,5) | 5 |

The two 132-orbits of PSL(2,11) are the Carmichael Steiner system S(5,6,12),
the orbit of {inf, 1, 3, 4, 5, 9} (infinity plus the nonzero squares of
F_11), and its mirror, the orbit of infinity plus the non-squares. The
non-square scaling z -> 2z, which lies in PGL(2,11) and not in PSL(2,11),
exchanges them. A PGL(2,11) shuffle would therefore destroy the secret; a
PSL(2,11) shuffle preserves it. This is the k = 5 form of the A5-on-6
chirality of the ladder note.

## 3. The two generators as card operations

Positions 0..11 from the top of a face-down pile of twelve cards, six hearts
and six clubs, cards of one colour indistinguishable.

**r4, reverse each quarter.** Split the pile into three piles of four, turn
each over as a block (or deal each into a new pile), restack in place.
Permutation (0 3)(1 2)(4 7)(5 6)(8 11)(9 10), an involution.

**m6, Monge-shuffle each half.** Split the pile into two piles of six,
perform the Monge up shuffle on each (top card to the hand, then alternately
on top and under), restack in place. Permutation (0 3 1 2 4 5)(6 9 7 8 10 11),
order 6. Its inverse is the milk shuffle of each half (Diaconis, Graham and
Kantor 1983, p. 193: the milk shuffle is the inverse of Monge's shuffle), so
the symmetrized alphabet {reverse quarters, Monge halves, milk halves} is
three named physical operations.

Both r4 and m6 lie in M12 = <Monge-12, reverse-12> of the M12 proposal, and
<r4, m6> is a PSL(2,11) inside it: the instance is the M12 shuffles applied to
sub-piles. Of the two Steiner systems, the orbit with representative
{0, 1, 3, 7, 10, 11} is the M12 hexad system and the orbit with representative
{2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9} is its mirror (verified: the M12-orbit of the first has 132
blocks and equals it, the M12-orbit of the second has 792). The M12 shuffle
moves the mirror decks out of the valid deck set, which is why the shuffle
group must stop at PSL(2,11).

## 4. Facts verified by exhaustive computation (2026-09-14)

Scratch `notes/probes/psl211_physical.py`; every claim is a full enumeration.

- |<r4, m6>| = 660, 2-transitive on the twelve positions, not 3-transitive.
- An explicit relabelling pi with pi <r4, m6> pi^-1 = PSL(2,11) acting on
  P^1(F_11) by Moebius maps: pi = (0,5,11,9,6,2,8,7,4,1,10,3) as a list of
  images of positions 0..11 (11 = infinity). Under pi the 132-orbit A is the
  Carmichael system, the orbit of {inf,1,3,4,5,9}.
- Orbits on 6-subsets: 110, 110, 110, 132, 132, 330 with design strengths
  3, 3, 3, 5, 5, 3.
- Colour view law of a coalition, fixed representative and uniform shuffle,
  equal across the two 132-classes for every coalition of size 1..5, unequal
  at size 6 (all coalitions checked).
- Ten reveals never determine the class: for every pair of hidden positions
  there is a block of each system agreeing on the other ten. Eleven reveals
  determine it, by colour pigeonhole. Ramp: private to 5, leak at 6,
  ambiguous through 10, determined at 11, decoder reads 12.
- Word walk to uniform on the 660 group elements: {r4, m6, m6^-1} reaches
  2^-40 at L = 570 (2^-13.9 at L = 200); the two-letter alphabet {r4, m6}
  reaches it at L = 406. Cayley diameter with the three letters: 12.
- In-kernel cost of the enumerated certificate: 660 x 3 x 570 = 1.1 x 10^6
  walk updates on numbers of about 900 bits, against 336 x 5 x 200 = 3.4 x
  10^5 on 464-bit numbers for pgl27_mixing.v, which takes at least 103 s
  (measured 2026-09-14 up to a syntax error from a concurrent edit). Estimate
  five to ten minutes. The closure table has 660 rows.
- No other physical operation from the pool of 520 (block reversals, cuts,
  Monge, faro, block swaps, parallel block operations) lies in <r4, m6>, so no
  third physical letter is available to speed the walk up.

## 5. What the Rocq instance would need beyond the pgl27 shape

- **Deck model.** Cards of one colour indistinguishable: the deck is a
  6-subset of positions (the heart set), the coalition view is H cap C, a
  finset rather than a finfun of codes. This is the Mizuki-Shizuya physical
  model and is new to the framework, which so far deals distinct codes. The
  all-decks dealer of transitivity_privacy.v (ttrans_view_indep_alldecks) is
  the existing point of contact: there the code view already factors through
  H cap C.
- **Privacy bridge.** Not ttrans_private. Either the design-theoretic lemma
  (intersection numbers of a t-design are determined by the parameters) or,
  cheaper, a literal check: for the two 132-row tables and every coalition of
  size <= 5 (1585 coalitions) and every A subset of C, the block counts agree
  (about 7 x 10^6 vm_compute steps). The view law is count / 132, so equal
  counts give equal laws.
- **Invariant.** Two 132-row tables; invariance under r4 and m6 by 264
  membership checks each, induction on words for the group.
- **Word model.** The pgl27_mixing.v shape at 660 states, three letters,
  L = 570 (or two letters, L = 406 if the non-symmetric alphabet is
  acceptable to the walk lemma).
- **Recovery.** Colour pigeonhole at 11; the 10-reveal ambiguity witness is
  the 1-swap pair of blocks, transported by 2-transitivity as in
  pgl27_recovery.v.

## 8. Soundness invariants

- No new axiom or assumed constant: every certificate is a vm_compute'd
  boolean over literal tables, as in pgl27_orbit.v and pgl27_mixing.v.
- The distributional privacy claims are exact equalities of laws (variation
  distance zero) in the exact-shuffle model and 2^-39-close laws in the word
  model; no computational assumption anywhere.
- Scope: coalitions are static position sets, fixed before the deal; the
  secret prior is arbitrary (`secretP : R.-fdist bool`); the dealer uses a
  fixed representative deck per class and a uniform (or L-word) shuffle; the
  observer is the colour-collapsed content, never the raw code view. The raw
  code view is NOT claimed independent for coalitions of size 3..5 and is
  false there.
- The bound goes vacuous nowhere: the group has 660 > 0 elements, both
  classes are populated (132 rows each), every coalition of size <= 5 is
  covered by the count check (1585 coalitions), size 6 is a proven leak.
- Cited library objects, one line each: `Gen_PGGTypes` (pgg_interface.v,
  section template over sigmas); `ThresholdScheme`, `ts_recon_perm_invariant`
  (pgg_sharing_framework.v); `ReconPlug` (covering_scheme.v, four fields);
  `MonodromyProfile` (pgg_monodromy_profile.v, four fields);
  `inde_prod_fst`, `coalition_view`, `dealt_secret`, `profile_view_indep`
  (transitivity_privacy.v); `fdistmap`, `fdist_uniform`/`` `U `` (infotheo
  fdist.v); `orbit`, `'C_G[x | to]`, `'P^*` (mathcomp action.v).
