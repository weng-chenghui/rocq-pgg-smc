# Proposal: the Mathieu group M12 as the second orbit-class instance (t = 5 rung)

Date: 2026-09-13

One-line summary: the twelve-card scheme shuffled by M12, the group generated by
the Monge shuffle and the pile reversal, is the natural second member of the
orbit-class secret-sharing family next to PGL(2,7). It has privacy threshold 5,
its secret is whether the six heart positions form a hexad of the Steiner
system S(5,6,12), and both generators are classical named physical shuffles.

Companion notes: the ladder report
[[20260701-175221-report-shuffle-matters-group-ladder]] (which names M11/M12 as
the open t = 4, 5 rung), the PGL(2,7) spec
[[20260702-114631-pgl27-orbit-class-ROCQ-formalization-spec]], and the PGL vs
Shamir explanation [[20260822-153515-pgl27-vs-shamir-protocol-explanations]].

## 1. The candidate

| | PGL(2,7) instance (landed) | M12 instance (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| card positions N | 8 = P^1(F_7) | 12 |
| group order | 336 | 95040 |
| transitivity | sharply 3-transitive | sharply 5-transitive |
| generators | 3: translation, scaling, inversion | 2: Monge shuffle u, reversal z |
| coloured cards | 4 hearts (codes < 4) | 6 hearts (codes < 6) |
| secret invariant | cross-ratio class of the heart 4-set | hexad membership of the heart 6-set |
| orbit split | 28 equianharmonic / 42 harmonic of 70 | 132 hexads / 792 non-hexads of 924 |
| privacy threshold ts_k' | 3 | 5 |
| first leaking coalition size | 4 | 6 |
| ambiguous through | 6 reveals | 10 reveals |
| determined at | 7 reveals (pigeonhole) | 11 reveals (pigeonhole) |
| reconstruction reads | all 8 | all 12 |

The recipe of the ladder report applies verbatim: a t-transitive but not
(t+1)-transitive group, privacy k = t, secret = orbit class of a (t+1)-subset.
M12 is the largest sharply multiply transitive group that exists (sharply
5-transitive on 12 points); above t = 5 the ladder ends by the classification
of multiply transitive groups, so this rung is the top of the family, not a
step in it.

## 2. The two generators as card operations

Positions are 0..11 from the top of a face-down pile. Cards are the twelve
codes 0..11; hearts are the codes 0..5.

**z, the reversal.** Deal the pile one card at a time onto the table into a
new pile. Position j goes to position 11 - j. As a permutation:
(0 11)(1 10)(2 9)(3 8)(4 7)(5 6), an involution.

**u, the Monge "up" shuffle** (Monge 1773; Diaconis, Graham and Kantor 1983,
Section 5). Hold the pile; take the top card into the other hand; then take
each next card from the top and place it alternately ON TOP of the new pile
and UNDER it, the second card going on top. For twelve cards the new pile,
read top to bottom, holds the old positions 11, 9, 7, 5, 3, 1, 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.
As a permutation of positions it is (0 6 9 1 5 3 4 8 10 11)(2 7), of order 10.
Diaconis, Graham and Kantor give it in closed form: position j goes to
floor(m/2) + (-1)^j floor((j+1)/2), with m = 12.

The theorem that makes this a shuffle group and not a pair of tricks is
Diaconis, Graham and Kantor 1983, p. 194: "for m = 12, <u, z> = M_12". The
same page shows that the Monge "down" shuffle is u followed by z, so the
alphabet {up shuffle, down shuffle} generates the same group and is the
alphabet a dealer would actually use (two named shuffles, no reversal step).

Compared with PGL(2,7), whose three generators are position-table operations
(cut the seven finite-point cards cyclically with the infinity card set aside;
rotate the six nonzero cards along the fixed 6-cycle 1 -> 3 -> 2 -> 6 -> 4 -> 5;
swap the four fixed pairs (0 inf)(1 6)(2 3)(4 5)), the M12 generators need no
position labels at all. A dealer performs them on an unlabelled pile by
counting only "top, under, top, under". That is the card-protocol content of
this instance: the shuffle group of the classical Monge shuffle, on a
twelve-card pile, is exactly the group whose 5-transitivity gives five-card
coalitions perfect privacy.

## 3. Facts verified by exhaustive computation (2026-09-13)

Scratch script `m12_check.py` and follow-ups, pure Python, BFS closure of
<u, z> on 12 points. All claims are full enumerations, not sampling.

- |<u, z>| = 95040 = 12 * 11 * 10 * 9 * 8.
- One orbit on ordered 5-tuples (5-transitive), seven orbits on ordered
  6-tuples (not 6-transitive). Sharpness: |G| equals the number of ordered
  5-tuples, so the 5-point stabiliser is trivial, exactly as for PGL(2,7) on
  triples; the re-dealing privacy witness (locked decision D5 of the PGL spec)
  transfers unchanged.
- Orbits on 6-subsets: sizes 132 and 792. The 132-orbit is a Steiner system
  S(5,6,12): every one of the 792 five-subsets lies in exactly one block. The
  complement of a hexad is a hexad. Both generators map the hexad table to
  itself (checked for all 132 rows).
- Every 6-set at swap distance one from a hexad is a non-hexad (two hexads
  meet in at most four points), which is the witness for "10 reveals never
  determine the class": for any two hidden positions, put a heart of a hexad
  at one and a club at the other, and the two decks obtained by swapping them
  have opposite classes while agreeing on the other ten positions.
- Cayley diameter of M12 with alphabet {u, u^-1, z}: 25; with {u, z}: 28.
- Schreier walk on the 12 positions with the symmetrized alphabet
  {u, u^-1, z}: second eigenvalue about 0.931 in absolute value (power
  iteration), against 7/8 for the PGL(2,7) five-letter alphabet. The per-seat
  endpoint bound sqrt(12) * 0.931^L becomes nontrivial from about L = 18.
- Comparison rows, same script: M11 = point stabiliser of M12 acting on the
  other 11 points is sharply 4-transitive (order 7920) with 5-subset orbits
  66 / 396 (the S(4,5,11) pentads); AGL(3,2) on 8 points (order 1344) is
  3-transitive, not 4-transitive, with 4-subset orbits 14 / 56 (the affine
  planes of F_2^3), and the in- and out-faro shuffles on 12 cards generate a
  group of order 7680 = 2^6 * 120, matching the DGK theorem's Z_2^6 x| PGL(2,5).

## 4. In-kernel certificate plan (what the Rocq instance would need)

The PGL(2,7) files certify 3-transitivity by a vm_compute BFS over 336 triples
with a seq-based visited list. At 95040 five-tuples that quadratic search is
out of reach, but the certificate does not have to enumerate the group.

- **5-transitivity by a transversal chain.** Base (0,1,2,3,4). For level i a
  table of words fixing base points 0..i-1 pointwise and carrying base point i
  to each point of its orbit. Measured sizes: orbits 12, 11, 10, 9, 8;
  longest words 4, 8, 12, 19, 20 letters; 50 words, 405 letters in total. The
  kernel checks each word by vm_compute on the nat tables (fixes the earlier
  base points, hits the claimed target); a generic lemma, provable once in
  transitivity_privacy.v and reusable for PGL(2,7) with 8 + 7 + 6 = 21 words,
  composes the five levels into `ntransitive 5`. The product 12 * 11 * 10 * 9 * 8
  of the orbit sizes is a by-product, not a premise.
- **The invariant as a table.** `hexad_tbl : seq (seq nat)` with 132 rows;
  `orbit_class sh := heart_set sh \in hexad_tbl`. Invariance under the
  generated group follows from invariance under u and z, 264 vm_compute
  membership checks, by induction on words; population of both classes is
  one witness deck per class. No closed-form invariant (no cross-ratio
  analogue) is needed, although one exists through the extended ternary Golay
  code or through PSL(2,11) on P^1(F_11).
- **Scheme record.** `ThresholdScheme bool 'I_12` with `ts_T' = 11`,
  `ts_k' = 5`, `ts_valid s sh := deck_ok sh /\ orbit_class sh = s`, privacy by
  `ttrans_private` at t = 5. The monodromy is `@Gen_PGGTypes 1 10 m12_gens`
  with a 2-tuple of permutations, rho the inclusion into S_12, mirroring D1
  of the PGL spec.
- **Mixing.** The exact-uniform model (dealer draws g uniformly from M12)
  needs no certificate. The word model needs a Schreier certificate on the
  12 x 12 transition matrix, the same rounded-LDL shape as
  pgl27_spectral_certificate.py at 12 x 12 instead of 8 x 8, with alpha
  about 0.94. The single-length enumerated bound of pgl27_mixing.v (walk on
  336 group elements at L = 200) does not transfer: the walk on 95040 group
  elements would have to be enumerated in the kernel. Either accept the
  weaker all-length Schreier bound or state the coalition bound over the
  exact model only.

## 5. Alternatives considered

- **M11 on 11 points**, sharply 4-transitive, order 7920, secret = pentad of
  S(4,5,11) (66 / 396). The t = 4 rung. It has no classical two-shuffle
  generation of its own; it is reached as the point stabiliser of M12, so the
  card operations would be "the M12 shuffles that leave the bottom card in
  place", which is not a physical operation. Reasonable as a derived
  instance after M12, not as the second instance.
- **AGL(3,2) on 8 points**, 3-transitive, order 1344, secret = affine plane
  of F_2^3 (14 / 56). Same deck size and threshold as PGL(2,7), different
  group and different orbit split; a good contrast pair but not a new rung.
  Generators (XOR-translations and linear maps of bit-triples) are
  table-driven, like PGL's.
- **PGL(2,q) for larger q** stays at t = 3 and only grows the deck.
- **PGammaL(2,8) on 9 points**, 3-transitive, order 1512, also stays at t = 3.
- **In and out faro shuffles on 12 cards** generate only Z_2^6 x| PGL(2,5),
  order 7680, which is imprimitive on the 12 positions and not 2-transitive;
  the faro alphabet is the wrong one for this deck size (DGK theorem (a)).

## 7. Addendum (same day): the word-shuffle certificate rules M12 out, AGL(3,2) in

Follow-up question: which candidate keeps an in-kernel enumerated walk
certificate (the shape of pgl27_mixing.v) at some computable word length,
with generators that are not the PGL translation / scaling / inversion triple.

Exact walk distances to uniform, symmetrized alphabets, computed on the full
group (scratch `notes/probes/small_candidates.py` and follow-ups):

- M12 with {u, u^-1, z}: 2^-6.1 at L = 200, about 0.038 bits per letter, so
  L close to 1000 for 2^-40; with {u, u^-1, d, d^-1}: 2^-10 at L = 200, L
  about 700. On 95040 states that is roughly 10^4 times the PGL(2,7) walk.
  Not computable in the present pipeline; M12 stays an exact-uniform-model
  instance only.
- PGL(2,q) rungs (q = 9, 11, 13): 720 / 1320 / 2184 states, L = 225 / 225 /
  245 for 2^-40. Computable, but the same generator triple.
- AGL(3,2) on 8 cards, order 1344, 3-transitive, 4-subset orbits 14 / 56:
  the 14-orbit is exactly the 4-subsets whose positions XOR to zero (the
  affine planes of F_2^3, the Steiner system S(3,4,8)). The faro shuffles
  alone generate only Z_2^3 x| Z_3 of order 24 (DGK theorem (e)); one local
  swap completes the group. Of 67 generating sets drawn from eleven physical
  operations, the best word lengths for 2^-40 are:

  | alphabet (symmetrized) | letters | L for 2^-40 |
  |---|---|---|
  | in-faro, reverse bottom half, 7-cycle fixing position 0 | 5 | 140 |
  | reverse bottom half, swap top pairs, 7-cycle | 4 | 210 |
  | in-faro, reverse top half, swap bottom pairs | 4 | 240 |
  | out-faro, reverse top half, swap bottom pairs | 4 | 350 |
  | out-faro, in-faro, swap bottom pairs | 5 | about 950 |

  The all-physical alphabet {in-faro, reverse the top half, swap the two
  bottom pairs} at L = 240 costs 1344 * 4 * 240 = 1.3 * 10^6 walk updates
  against 3.4 * 10^5 for pgl27_mixing.v, about four times. The 7-cycle
  fixing position 0 is the Singer cycle of F_8 and, like the PGL letters,
  needs a position table; the faro, reversal and pair-swap letters do not.

Recommendation under the word-certificate constraint: AGL(3,2) on the same
eight-card deck as PGL(2,7), secret "the four heart positions XOR to zero",
alphabet {in-faro, reverse top half, swap bottom pairs}, L = 240. The two
instances then differ only in the group and the invariant, which makes the
comparison clean: same deck, same threshold 3, 14 / 56 against 28 / 42, and
the point stabiliser GL(3,2) of AGL(3,2) is abstractly isomorphic to
PSL(2,7), the index-two subgroup of the other instance's group.
