# P8 landing plan: the relations between two security properties' propositions

Tracker step 3.4. Spec with the probe's and the two audits' results folded in:
`notes/20260920-p8-arm-relations-probe-design.md`, last section (it decides
where this plan is silent). Probe (the verbatim source of every proof):
`notes/probes/2026-09-20-p8-arm-relations/` (`p8a_degenerate_certificate.v`,
`p8b_proximity_from_indistinguishability.v`, `p8b_instances.v`, `LEDGER.md`,

Vocabulary (owner, 2026-09-20): a program certifies a SECURITY PROPERTY with
SECURITY EVIDENCE; the framework names are `SecurityEvidence`, `SecurityProperty`,
`EvidenceProp`, `security_property_of`. Two nouns are barred in every artifact
(`notes/probes/2026-09-20-security-property-rename/prose_sheet.md`): none of the

audits predate the rule; text copied from them is rewritten by the sheet's verb
rule (evidence holds fields and proves a proposition; a property is a name; a
proposition states and compares; a tail lemma derives; a program certifies and
publishes).

## Decisions, each with its reason

1. **Homes.** General lemmas on laws: `lib/var_dist_supp.v` (reverse closure 39
   files, no frozen file). Independence and product-law lemmas: a NEW file
   `lib/fdist_prod_cond.v`, not `lib/proba_entropy_ext.v`, whose reverse closure
   is 57 files for one three-line lemma. The general core of the closeness
   step: `security/var_dist_joint_law.v`. Everything that names a certificate:
   `manifest/pgg_tableau_security_property_relations.v` (a leaf: nothing imports
   it). Instance facts: the model files for the product hypothesis
   (`instances/pgl27/pgl27_models.v`, `instances/kim2025/five_card_models.v`;
   neither imports a tableau or manifest module and that stays so), the
   analysis-bridged files for the comparisons of numbers.
2. **`var_dist P P = 0`.** Named `var_dist_xx` (MathComp's `xx` for a reflexive
   instance) in `lib/var_dist_supp.v`. `legacy/security/pgg_uniform_security.v`
   declares `var_dist_self` in the same namespace; legacy is not edited and the
   two names do not collide.
3. **The certificate over every model** is named by what it is: its two secrets
   are the constants true and false. `exact_witness_cst_true`,
   `idealproximity_cert_cst_secrets_true_false`. No value judgement in a name
   or a comment.
4. **Headline A is premise-free and parameter-free.** `profile_k` is a successor
   at every algebra, so `0 < profile_k` is proved inside
   (`profile_k_gt0`, from the audit's scratch file). Landed:
   `idealproximity_prop_cst_secrets_lt2_false` (for `c < 2` the proposition of
   that certificate is false), its endpoint `idealproximity_prop_cst_secrets_at2`
   is NOT needed (it is `idealproximity_prop_at2`), the universal
   `idealproximity_prop_lt2_uniform_in_cert_false : c < 2 -> ~ forall cert,
   IdealProximityPropAt cert c`, and ONE premise-shaped corollary,
   `indistinguishability_prop_idealproximity_lt2_false`. The `Q`-parametrised
   form is not landed (it is the universal restated).
5. **Section B takes the certificate itself.** Variables `R A E sa`, `argT`,
   `arg_decode`, `arg_read`, `ic : IndistinguishabilityCert sa`; hypotheses
   `Harg : forall u, sa.(sa_arg) u = arg_decode (arg_read u)` and
   `Hprod : fdistmap (fun u => (arg_read u, sa.(sa_cut) u)) (sa_sampleP sa)
            = fdistmap arg_read (sa_sampleP sa) \`x sa_cut_dist sa`.
   `Parg`, `rho`, `rho0`, `Hic`, `Hconst` of the probe disappear: they are
   determined (`fdist_prod1`, `sa_cut_dist_of_prodE`, `ic_ideal ic`,
   `ic_const ic`). The threshold premise the closeness field discards is
   dropped. Names as the naming audit's list, with `prod` for `product`.
6. **B5's premise** `instance_endpoints_stmt E` stays an explicit hypothesis of
   the headline `idealproximity_prop_of_indistinguishability`: every program at
   `Sampled` holds it (`sp_He`), so a user inside a program has it; a user
   outside supplies it. Its comment says that the statement's strength is the
   fineness of `arg_read` and that at a constant reading it is true and says
   nothing; `arg_read_distE` (the secret has one law on both sides) lands
   beside it.
7. **One `Arguments` line per landed lemma of section B**, verified by
   elaborating an application (not by `Check`): the probe lost three compiles to
   arguments that discharge made implicit.
8. **Instances.** `pgl27_word_arg_cut_prodE`, `pgl27_word_arg_readE`,
   `kim_biased_arg_cut_prodE`, `kim_biased_arg_readE` with the factors NAMED (no
   existential). Numbers: `pgl27_word_proximity_eps_sw_boundE` (equal, 2^-40);
   at the five-card one-cut model two facts: over `kim_biased_cert_exact` the
   epsilon is one fiftieth, equal to the landed proximity certificate's
   (`erefl`-closed in the audit's scratch file), and over `kim_biased_cert` it is
   sqrt 5 / 80, which is larger (`kim_biased_proximity_eps_le_sw_bound`). The
   probe ledger's "departure 6" is withdrawn in the landed comments: the number
   is whichever epsilon the chosen certificate carries.
9. **Not landed:** the lemma restating `cert_eps`, the double-negation mutation,
   `Search` and `Print Assumptions` commands, the unused `erefl` lemma, the
   decomposition file (it stays a probe).
10. **The home file's header.** Its "Not claimed." paragraph and index are
    rewritten to what is claimed after this landing: the universal refutation
    below two (from one certificate over every model), and the construction of
    ideal-proximity evidence from input-indistinguishability evidence under the
    product hypothesis. It keeps saying what is still not claimed: no
    implication at a given, instance-chosen proximity certificate, and nothing
    for a model whose cut depends on its run argument.
